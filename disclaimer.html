This page is free, has no account and sells nothing, so there is no incentive here to overstate what it can do. What follows is a plain account of where the arithmetic ends and the interpretation begins, what the calculator cannot know, and what you should not use it for.

The short version: the eight characters are computed and checkable, everything said about them is a traditional framework rather than a finding, nothing here predicts events, and any decision you make after reading it is yours.

What is actually computed

The eight characters are arithmetic. Given a birth moment they are as determinate as a calendar date: the sexagenary cycles, the solar terms solved from the sun's apparent longitude, and the classical derivation rules for each stem and branch. The calculator shows its working in the Calculation detail panel so you can check every step against an almanac or another calculator.

That part is verifiable, and we stand behind it. Where it cannot settle something, such as a birth sitting within minutes of a solar term or one whose time zone is unknown, it says so on the page and shows both possible readings rather than quietly picking one.

What is interpretation

Everything after the characters is interpretation: the Day Master descriptions, the Ten God glosses, the element commentary, and the way the luck and annual pillars are described.

This is a traditional interpretive framework with a long history. It is not a scientific claim. It has not been tested against outcomes, there is no evidence that it predicts anything, and it should not be treated as a finding about you or about the future. Read it as a description of tendencies that may or may not ring true, and give it exactly as much weight as it earns.

Accuracy has limits

A chart is only as good as what goes into it. Any of the following will change the result, sometimes substantially:

  • A misremembered or rounded birth time. Two hours makes a different hour pillar.
  • An unlisted birthplace, which means no true solar time correction.
  • An unrecorded daylight saving shift, which is a full hour and can move the hour pillar.
  • A birth close to a solar term, where the month and sometimes the year pillar turn.
  • Historical time zone changes that no lookup table here models.

Beyond the inputs, the model itself is deliberately partial. Branch combinations and clashes are not modelled, the seasonal strength of the Day Master is not weighted, and there is no useful god. The guide sets out these limits in detail. Solar term instants are accurate to about five minutes, and the range is 1901 to 2100.

Nothing here is generated, and nothing leaves your browser

The interpretation text is a fixed lookup table written in advance. It is not AI output, and it does not change between visitors with the same chart.

There is no server, no account, no analytics, no cookies and no network request. Your birth details are used inside the page and forgotten when you close the tab. The one thing to be aware of: a shared chart link carries the birth details in its own web address, so a link is as private as wherever you paste it. Treat one like any other personal link.

If a reading is distressing

Some traditional language is blunt. Terms like "Seven Killings" and "Rob Wealth" are literal translations of old names, not warnings, and no combination in this system means harm is coming to you.

If anything here leaves you anxious about your health, your future, your relationships or your safety, please close the page and speak to a doctor, a counsellor, or someone you trust. A chart is a description, not a diagnosis and not a fate. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country rather than looking for an answer here.
