Your Day Master 日主, the character that represents you

Why does one character represent me?

Of your eight characters, one is you: the stem of the day pillar. Everything else in the chart is then read in relation to it, which is why the same character means different things in two different people’s charts.

Read the image literally and it will take you further than you expect. 甲 is the tall tree: it grows in one direction and needs room. 乙 is the vine: same element, opposite strategy, it goes around obstacles and climbs what is already there.

Four Pillars 四柱

Luck Pillars 大运

Ten-year periods laid over your chart. Select one to read it.

What are luck pillars?

Your eight characters are fixed for life. The luck pillars are what moves across them: a run of ten-year periods, each with a stem and branch of its own. The chart says what you are; the luck pillar says what season you are in.

Each is labelled with its Ten God, which is its relationship to you. That label is the shortest useful summary of the decade, and selecting a pillar spells it out underneath.

Five Elements 五行

What your chart is made of, and how the five elements act on each other.

How do I read this?

The five elements are only interesting because of how they act on each other. Each one generates the next around the circle and controls the one across from it.

Fix your own element in place and those relationships get names. What you generate is Output, what you control is Wealth, what controls you is Authority, and what generates you is Resource. That is the whole of the Ten Gods system in one picture.

Ten Gods 十神

Everything else in your chart, named by its relationship to you.

What are the Ten Gods?

Every character in your chart other than you is labelled by how it relates to you, rather like a family tree drawn around one person. There are five relationships, each in a milder and a more intense flavour, which is where ten comes from.

The names are old and dramatic. Seven Killings means pressure without a formal relationship softening it, such as a deadline or a rival. Hurting Officer means talent that will not sit inside rules. None of the ten are good or bad.
